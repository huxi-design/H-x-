Modular shelving — concept

Yohaku

Yohaku is the white margin left empty on a page or a painting. In Japanese aesthetics it is not a lack but a presence: the empty part lets the rest breathe.

Concept

Yohaku is a modular shelving system built from a single element in plywood, repeated. Like Mana, the modules slot together without screws or tools, and can be taken apart and rebuilt in a different form.

Each module can be left open or filled. The bookcase is composed as much by its voids as by the objects it holds, and the empty spaces become part of the design.

Yohaku grows with whoever uses it, one module at a time: a piece of furniture that changes with the person, instead of asking the person to adapt to it.
