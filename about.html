About

Hūxī is a product design studio working in interior design — seating, work surfaces and custom furniture.

Our work is defined by a clean, clear design language that still tries to step outside the margins. We believe the beauty of an object comes from the beauty of the idea as it is developed on the ground, step by step, through the making.

Building an object can take months. The care lives in that time — until the result carries whoever lives with it into their own ideal space.
