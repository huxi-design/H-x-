About

Hūxī

Hūxī is a furniture and object design studio founded by Francesco Carta. The name comes from the Chinese 呼吸, hūxī, which means breath.

Studio

Breath is the rhythm the studio looks for in every project: objects that leave room, that slow things down, that help people inhabit a space with more presence.

We design seating, work surfaces and furniture with a clean, essential language, starting from the idea and developing it in the field, with constant attention to materials and to the way things are made.
